## Why Grimsby Converter

Need a FLAC album as MP3 for your phone, a MOV from an iPhone as MP4, HEIC photos as JPG and a DOCX as PDF?
Usually that means four different websites, uploading private files to someone else's server and waiting in line.

**Grimsby Converter does it in one window.** Drop in files and folders in any mix — they are sorted into groups,
and each group gets its own format and quality. One button, and everything is converted on your computer.

## Features

### 📥 Drop everything at once

Drag files or whole folders into the window — video, music, photos and documents mixed together.
Grimsby sorts them into groups by type and shows how many files are in each.

![Files sorted into groups](screenshots/queue.jpg)

### 🎚 Each group gets its own settings

Pick the target format and quality per group: say, video to MP4, music to MP3 320 kbps, photos to WEBP.

- **Video — "Fast" mode:** if the codec fits the new container, the video is simply repackaged
  (MOV → MP4 in seconds, with zero quality loss). If not, it's re-encoded to H.264/AAC.
- **Audio:** tags (artist, album, title, year) carry over to the new files.
- **Photos:** HEIC from iPhone, AVIF, WEBP, SVG — everything opens and saves in the usual formats, even to PDF.
- **Documents, spreadsheets, presentations** → PDF, DOCX, XLSX and more.

### ⚡ Fast and safe

- Several files are converted at the same time — as many as your processor can handle.
- **Originals are never touched.** The new file is written next to the original (or to a folder you choose)
  and appears only once it's fully ready — no half-written files.
- For every finished file you see how much space it saved, and a "Show" button opens it in Explorer.

## Formats

| Group | Opens | Saves to |
|---|---|---|
| 🎬 Video | MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WEBM, WMV, FLV, M4V, 3GP, MTS, M2TS, TS, MPG, VOB | MP4, MKV, WEBM, MOV, AVI, GIF; sound only → MP3, AAC, WAV |
| 🎵 Audio | MP3, FLAC, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, WAV, WMA, APE, WV, AIFF, AMR, MKA | MP3, AAC, FLAC, WAV, OGG, Opus |

> If it isn't installed, Grimsby will offer to download it. Video, audio and photos work right away.

## Download and install

1. Open the **[latest release](https://github.com/maksimkhatskevich/grimsby-converter/releases/latest)** page and download the installer `.exe`.
2. Run it. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" window — that's a normal warning for apps
   without a paid code-signing certificate. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Drop your files into the window. That's it.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
